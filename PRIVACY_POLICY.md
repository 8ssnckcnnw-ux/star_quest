# Privacy Policy for Star Quest

**Effective Date**: 2024

## 1. Introduction

Star Quest ("the App") is a free educational game for children. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### We DO NOT Collect:
- Personal information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Device identifiers (IDFA, Android Advertising ID)
- User behavior analytics or crash reports
- Third-party tracking
- Cookies or similar tracking technologies

### We DO Store Locally (On Your Device Only):
- Game progress (stars, levels, badges)
- Selected character
- Audio/music preferences
- No data is sent to our servers

## 3. Data Storage

All game data is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This data:
- Never leaves your device
- Is not shared with anyone
- Can be deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling the app
- Is not backed up to cloud services

## 4. Third-Party Services

Star Quest does not use:
- Firebase or similar analytics
- Google Analytics
- Crashlytics
- Social media integrations
- Advertisement networks
- Payment processors (no in-app purchases)

## 5. Children's Privacy

Star Quest is designed for children and complies with:
- **COPPA** (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation)
- **CCPA** (California Consumer Privacy Act)

We do not collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 6. Permissions

Star Quest only requires:
- Storage access (to save game progress)
- No camera access
- No microphone access
- No contact access
- No location access

## 7. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted in-app with a new effective date.

## 8. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us through your app store.

---

**Star Quest is committed to protecting your privacy.** We believe games for children should be safe, fun, and free from data collection.
